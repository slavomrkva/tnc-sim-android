The screenshots are current Android captures. The recording status bar and
Android system navigation bar were removed, then the undistorted app image was
centered on a matching dark 9:16 canvas. Upload them in the numbered order.

The preview video shows the Editor and custom keyboard, 3D simulation, Learn
theory and a practice task. Upload it to YouTube as Public or Unlisted with
monetization and age restriction off and embedding allowed. Paste the plain
video URL into Play Console without playlist or time-code parameters.

Suggested screenshot alt text:

1. TNC Sim editor showing a CNC program and guided path-function controls.
2. 3D machining simulation with the tool, workpiece and colored toolpaths.
3. Finished simulated workpiece with drilled and milled features.
4. Toolpath-only 3D view showing programmed machining movements.
5. Learn section with interactive Heidenhain programming lessons.
